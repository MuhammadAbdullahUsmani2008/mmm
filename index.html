Who We Are

A Brotherhood of Healthcare Professionals in Service of Humanity

Muslim Medical Mission began as a group of Muslim healthcare professionals working on shared concerns — free medical camping, free health-care clinics and health-awareness seminars — with a core mission of promoting a better understanding of Islam and medical practice within the framework of Islam.

When the devastating earthquake struck northern Pakistan in October 2005, it became the inciting stimulus that organised these professionals into Muslim Medical Mission (MMM). Today we impart educational and research programs, training sessions, seminars, symposia and specialist lectures — alongside humanitarian services such as relief operations, welfare clinics and surgical camps for those in need, without discrimination of race & religion.

  • Evidence-based medical & religious practice
  • Service under the guidance of the Quran & Sunnah
  • SECP-registered not-for-profit organization
Our Mission

“Evidence-Based Professionalism”

To become dynamic Muslim health-care professionals who pioneer social change by acquiring knowledge and wisdom, and practicing Dawah with acumen and convincing communication — orienting health education with Islamic values and their application in patient care.

Our Vision

“Sibghat-Ullah”

“Know therefore that there is no god but Allah, and ask forgiveness for your fault and for the believing men and believing women.”— Surah Muhammad, 47:19

In our professional, moral and spiritual progress, we seek Allah’s help and guidance to develop the ability to achieve the shades of ‘Sibghat-Ullah’.

Our Means

Service Without Discrimination

Service to humanity without discrimination of gender or genetics, race or religion, geography or generation, time or era. Human-resource development for peace and disaster — developing and practicing medical science and technology for peace.

What We Do

Medical & Humanitarian Programs

From welfare clinics in remote villages to surgical camps and disaster relief — every program is delivered by healthcare professionals, for the sake of Allah alone.

01

Free Medical Camps

Free medical camping for underserved communities — from Tharparkar to disaster-hit valleys — bringing doctors, diagnosis and essential medicines to the doorstep of those in need.

02

Welfare Clinics

Community welfare clinics offering accessible, compassionate health care guided by evidence-based practice and Islamic ethics of patient care.

03

Surgical Camps

Organised surgical camps that restore health and dignity for patients who cannot afford or access operative care.

04

Health Awareness Seminars

Public health-awareness seminars and programs for the general public — empowering families with knowledge of health and well-being.

05

Disaster & Emergency Relief

Rapid relief operations for earthquakes, floods, droughts and epidemics — food, shelter support and medical aid, without discrimination.

06

Education & Training

Training sessions, seminars, symposia and specialist lectures for healthcare professionals of all medical fields — bridging medical practice and Islamic values.

Services in Disasters

When Calamity Strikes, We Answer

Earthquakes, windstorms, floods and epidemics strike anywhere on earth without warning. Since its inception, Muslim Medical Mission has always tried to help those affected by such disasters — irrespective of any discrimination.

October 2005

Pakistan Earthquake — The Founding Response

The devastating earthquake in northern Pakistan became the inciting stimulus that organised Muslim healthcare professionals into Muslim Medical Mission, mobilising medical relief for survivors.

2015

MMM teams responded to flood-affected families in Chitral with relief support and free medical care in the aftermath of the disaster.
